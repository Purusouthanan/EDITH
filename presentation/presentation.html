SLIDE 03 / 16
1. Demand Surges & Depletion
ACUTE RISK

Floods and cyclones cause sudden, localized spikes in trauma, drowning, electrocution, waterborne contagions, and hypothermia across relief centres.

Supplies Depleted in Hours: Medical oxygen cylinders, O-negative blood units, tetanus toxoid, pediatric IV saline, and generator diesel exhaust rapidly with zero visibility into regional backups.
2. Fragmented Channel Chaos
COORDINATION GAP

Hospitals and medical camps broadcast desperate appeals across disjointed WhatsApp groups, SMS threads, informal phone calls, and paper slips.

Fatal Distribution Asymmetry: A high-profile urban shelter receives redundant donations from 4 different donors, while an isolated medical camp 5 km away receives nothing.
3. Severed Infrastructure
NETWORK DROP

High cyclone winds topple mobile towers; flooded electrical substations plunge districts into darkness; fiber backhauls are severed by uprooted trees.

Cloud SaaS Fragility: Standard enterprise hospital management systems, EHRs, and heavy SaaS dashboards freeze or crash the moment 4G/5G drops.

SECTION C · INTEGRITY & SECURITY
Data Integrity, Security & Concurrency Controls
Engineering safeguards that eliminate race conditions, unauthorized actions, and data corruption.
SLIDE 10 / 16
1. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
AUTHORITY BOUNDARIES

Permissions are enforced strictly by backend API middleware and database foreign key checks, never merely hidden in the UI:

  • Hospital / Camp Users: Scoped strictly to drafting requests and voluntary offers for their own registered facility.
  • ICC Coordinators: Global zone authority to acknowledge, triage, approve allocations, and close tickets.
  • Logistics Drivers: Scoped strictly to updating shipment progress and logging cargo drop-off.
2. Atomic Allocation & Race Prevention
ACID ROW LOCKS

Disaster Scenario: Two coordinators simultaneously attempt to allocate the final 10 oxygen cylinders from Hospital Beta to two different camps.

SELECT quantity_available FROM offers WHERE id = :id FOR UPDATE;
-- Row is locked; Coordinator A commits 10; remaining becomes 0.
-- Coordinator B's lock releases; detects 0 available; gracefully rejected with 409 Conflict.
Guarantees zero physical over-allocation under peak crisis concurrency.
3. Idempotency & Duplicate Prevention
SAFE REPLAY

When unstable cellular links drop mid-transmission, automated client retries can accidentally spawn duplicate tickets:

  • Every mutation payload generates a client-side UUID v4 X-Idempotency-Key.
  • The backend verifies this key against the processed_operations table before execution.
  • Repeated requests return the cached original response without creating duplicate records.
4. Two-Phase Fulfilment Verification
NON-REPUDIATION

Dispatching relief cargo is not proof of arrival:

  • Driver logs physical drop-off with vehicle ID and timestamp at the destination gate.
  • Resolution is NOT triggered: Ticket remains in DELIVERED_PENDING_VERIFICATION.
  • The receiving medical officer must log in, inspect cargo, and verify physical count.
